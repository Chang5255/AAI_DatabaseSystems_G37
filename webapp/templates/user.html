Deleting removes the user's ratings through ON DELETE CASCADE in MySQL. MongoDB has no foreign keys, so the app deletes their tags, activity and recommendations itself — and because the two databases cannot share a transaction, it snapshots the MongoDB documents first, holds the MySQL delete open until the MongoDB delete succeeds, and puts the documents back if the commit then fails. A compensating transaction, not an atomic one.
