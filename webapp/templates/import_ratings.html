What the two modes do differently

-- Per line                             -- All or nothing
START TRANSACTION;                      START TRANSACTION;
  SAVEPOINT line_0;                       INSERT ... (line 1)   -- ok
  INSERT ... (line 1)   -- ok             INSERT ... (line 2)   -- ok
  SAVEPOINT line_1;                       INSERT ... (line 3)   -- REFUSED
  INSERT ... (line 2)   -- REFUSED      ROLLBACK;               -- lines 1-2 lost too
  ROLLBACK TO SAVEPOINT line_1;
  SAVEPOINT line_2;
  INSERT ... (line 3)   -- ok
COMMIT;                 -- lines 1, 3 kept

ROLLBACK TO SAVEPOINT undoes the statements after the savepoint and releases the locks they took, but it does not end the transaction: the earlier lines stay uncommitted, and still locked, until the final COMMIT.
