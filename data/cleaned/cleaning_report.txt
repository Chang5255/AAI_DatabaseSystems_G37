ADDENDUM - the ratings are no longer synthetic
added 2026-11-10
============================================================

The pass above generated 12 users and 784 ratings, because TMDB
publishes only an aggregate vote_average and never a per-user rating.
784 rows was too few to demonstrate the analytics this project is
built around, so a second pass brought in real data:

  data/build_movielens_ratings.py   the code
  data/cleaned/movielens_report.txt the audit trail, in the same
                                    format as this one

Source: MovieLens 25M (GroupLens, University of Minnesota), joined to
our TMDB catalogue on links.csv tmdbId - the same key our two
databases already share, so no title matching was needed.
Cite: Harper and Konstan, ACM TiiS 5(4), 2015, doi 10.1145/2827872.

WHAT CHANGED
  users.csv     12 ->      1,512 rows   12 synthetic + 1,500 real
  ratings.csv  784 ->    246,976 rows  784 synthetic + 246,192 real
  user_tags.jsonl          72,600 docs  REAL MovieLens tag applications
  user_activity_logs.jsonl  9,797 docs  SYNTHETIC, derived from the
                                        real ratings (no public dataset
                                        contains page views)

WHAT DID NOT CHANGE
  * the schema: every value satisfies the constraints that were already
    in 01_schema.sql, including the 0.5-5.0 range and the half-star
    step, which MovieLens happens to use natively
  * the catalogue: movies, genres, people, movie_cast, movie_crew,
    movie_genres and movie_features.jsonl are untouched
  * the 12 synthetic users (ids 1-12) and their 784 ratings, kept as
    they are because they are the accounts the UI demos with and were
    designed to exercise the constraints

PROVENANCE IS CHECKABLE IN SQL
  user_id <= 1000  synthetic, from the pass above
  user_id >  1000  real MovieLens user (username ml_<their id>)
  03_verify.sql section C5 prints the split.

DENSITY AFTER THE CHANGE
  films with at least one rating    : 4,169 of 4,803
  films with at least 20 ratings    : 2,201   (the old target was 1,000)
  ratings per film  : median 22, mean 59
  ratings per user  : min 20, mean 163, max 2,148
  real ratings span : 1996-04-05 to 2019-11-21

The sampling design, and the reasons for it, are in
movielens_report.txt. The short version: 1,000 users drawn uniformly
from everyone with 20+ ratings on our films, plus 500 drawn from those
who also wrote 5+ tags, because the MongoDB tag collection needed real
volume. The second stratum means the tag data is NOT a uniform sample
of MovieLens taggers, and that is stated rather than hidden.
