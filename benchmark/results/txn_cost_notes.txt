innodb_flush_log_at_trx_commit = 1 means the log is flushed and
fsynced at every commit, which is what makes variant 1 slow: the
cost is one disk sync per row. Variant 2 pays it once. Variant 3
also removes round trips. The MySQL and MongoDB figures are not a
head-to-head race: different operations, different guarantees.
